Appendix

From June assumptions to July reality

Who pays
We assumed journalists would buy it. The institutions around them pay: states, agencies and funders.
The customer
We assumed one archetype, the investigative reporter. We found four segments sharing one need.
What they buy
We assumed a tool for one-off investigations. Buyers pay for measurement over time, and they renew on it.
The price
We assumed a flat subscription. Buyers wanted a build fee, a monthly tier and metered credits.
