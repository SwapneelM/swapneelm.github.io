We trace each claim to its first post and everyone who repeated it

One narrative holds the X accounts and the Bluesky accounts together, so a claim that jumps platforms stays one thread.

01 · MAP

Themes show who amplifies whom, in which language, on which platform.

02 · TRACE THE CLAIM

Each claim links back to the post it came from and to every account that posted the same words. In one US narrative last week we traced 7 claims, and 66 pairs of accounts posted the same text.

03 · ASK

Ask a question and the agent writes and runs the analysis. Answers come back in under a minute.

04 · CHECK

Every row opens into its posts. We hand-graded 540 agent runs, and an answer is accepted only when its code reproduces our reference analysis.

Case study Elections: Maryland's mail-in ballots

What officially happened

A printing error sent some voters the wrong primary ballot. Replacements and unique envelopes rule out duplicate voting.

state board explanation, cross-platform

What the feed made of it

The claim: 500,000 fake ballots. False per the election administrator and fact-checkers.
Rated false
The claim: a million ballots circulating. Originals plus replacements, recast as duplicates.
Escalation
One sentence, 50+ accounts. The same wording appeared across platforms within hours.
Identical wording

The top post, 1.32M interactions, demanded a new federal voting law. Maryland’s election administrator signed a letter of commitment to deploy statewide.
