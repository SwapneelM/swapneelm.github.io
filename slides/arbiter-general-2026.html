Case study Elections: Maryland's mail-in ballots

What officially happened

A printing error sent some voters the wrong primary ballot. Replacements and unique envelopes rule out duplicate voting.

state board explanation, cross-platform

The official explanation, cited in the study.

What the feed made of it

The claim: 500,000 fake ballots. False per the election administrator and fact-checkers.
Rated false
The claim: a million ballots circulating. Originals plus replacements, recast as duplicates.
Escalation
One sentence, 50+ accounts. The same wording appeared across platforms within hours.
Identical wording

The top post, 1.32M interactions, demanded a new federal voting law. Maryland’s election administrator signed a letter of commitment to deploy statewide.
